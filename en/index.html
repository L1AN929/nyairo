Questions & Honest Boundaries

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers on hardware needs, pricing, privacy, world customization, and safe upgrades.

Troubleshooting →
Do I need a dedicated GPU to run nyairo smoothly?
No. When connected to cloud APIs such as DeepSeek, OpenAI, Claude, or Qwen, model inference runs remotely while your computer only runs the ~6.0 MB desktop client and local SQLite memory ledger. Any everyday laptop with integrated graphics runs it quietly in the system tray. (A local GPU is only helpful if you choose to run local Ollama models or optional local image generation.)
Are my chats, SOUL.md persona, or memories ever uploaded to nyairo servers?
Never. nyairo operates zero centralized cloud servers. Your SOUL.md persona, full conversation database, long-term memory lantern, and co-reading drafts live strictly inside ~/.nyairo on your own machine. Network requests go solely to the model endpoint or messaging channel you configure — and stay 100% offline with local Ollama. See Privacy & Data Boundaries.
Does everyone live in the exact same town, or can I generate and edit my own world?
nyairo bundles curated worlds (coastal Nagiura, hot-spring Onsawa, rural Inobe, suburban Wakaba, and more), supports one-click template-free procedural exclusive town generation so your road layout and shop roster are uniquely yours, and includes an offline visual map editor to draw walls, place doors, arrange furniture, and export custom world packs.
Is nyairo free? Do I need an account or monthly subscription?
nyairo is completely free and open-source under Apache-2.0, with zero nyairo accounts, paid tiers, or token markups. Simply paste your own API key (a ~$1 top-up on DeepSeek lasts weeks of everyday conversation) or connect a free local Ollama instance. Autonomous background wakeups are disabled by default and guarded by strict daily caps.
How is nyairo different from standard local AI chat frontends or roleplay wrappers?
In ordinary chat boxes, time freezes when you close the tab and locations are improvised text. In nyairo, your companion inhabits a deterministic road graph and room floorplan: walking to a bookstore takes real minutes, non-urgent pings wait in their phone reply tray while walking, hot tea cools naturally on the desk, and they keep grounded everyday refusal boundaries without corporate apology scripts.
Will updating or uninstalling the desktop app erase my companion's memories?
No. Read-only application binaries and personal ~/.nyairo data are strictly separated. Uninstalling the Windows desktop client never touches your memory database in WSL 2, CLI upgrades support nyairo update --rollback, and .nyairo-suitcase.zip lets you pack and migrate your entire world and memories losslessly with API keys stripped.
